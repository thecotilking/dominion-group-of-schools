About Dominion

Our story, our values, and the people who make Dominion feel like home.

Our Story

Founded on care, built on consistency

Dominion Group of Schools was founded in 2002 by Rev. Esther Ebolum, with a simple calling: to raise children in knowledge, wisdom and the fear of God — from their very first day of Creche to their final year of Secondary school. (Founding year to be confirmed — update if needed.)

Our Mission

To provide a purely Christian education that builds confident, disciplined and knowledgeable young people, raised to become future leaders in their generation.

Our Vision

To be a leading Christian group of schools known for academic excellence, godly character, and the raising up of tomorrow's leaders.

Our Values

Knowledge, wisdom and the fear of God — the same three pillars on the Dominion crest, lived out in every classroom.
