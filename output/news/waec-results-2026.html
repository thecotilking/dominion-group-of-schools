Our First Set Shines in WASSCE!

Dominion College, Asaba celebrated a proud milestone this year: our very first graduating set sat WASSCE and delivered outstanding results, with multiple straight A1 distinctions across Marketing, Economics, Government, Christian Religious Studies and more. Congratulations to Egwuchukwu Divine Favour, Obaze Udoka Johnbosco, Okpako Runor Joshua and Okwudibonye Nkechi Juliana for leading the way as Dominion's first set of WASSCE candidates. This is the same standard of excellence and Christian character every Dominion student is raised on, from Nursery through Secondary. Ready to give your child the same strong foundation?
