Cultural Day at Dominion Group of Schools

Dominion Group of Schools comes alive with color, music, and tradition as we celebrate Cultural Day! This vibrant annual event brings our students together to showcase the rich diversity of Nigeria’s cultures through traditional attire, dance, music, and cuisine. Pupils will parade in colorful outfits representing various ethnic groups, perform cultural dances and songs, and take part in exhibitions highlighting our nation’s heritage.

Cultural Day is more than a celebration — it’s a hands-on lesson in unity, identity, and respect for the diverse tapestry that makes up our community. Parents and guardians are warmly invited to join us for a day filled with pride, learning, and fun.

Come celebrate with us as we honor where we come from and the values that bind us together!
